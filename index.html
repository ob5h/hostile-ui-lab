Password standard NS-14.2

Password requirements

  1. Passwords are composed of three approved dictionary words.
  2. Each word is selected by its numeric index from the approved list.
  3. A two-digit number is required.
  4. Exactly one approved symbol is required.
  5. Direct keyboard entry is disabled to prevent typographical errors.
  6. Word order matters.
  7. Repeated words are permitted, but discouraged by a tooltip we did not build.
  8. Numbers from 00 through 99 are supported.
  9. Symbols are selected from a twelve-character compliance set.
  10. Copying is permitted after generation.
  11. Pasting back into this form is unnecessary because the form already knows it.
  12. Changing any slider immediately changes the password.
  13. The resulting password is valid even if you dislike it.
  14. This policy is intentionally optimized for machines, not people.
